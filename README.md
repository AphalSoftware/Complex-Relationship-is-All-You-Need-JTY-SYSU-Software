# 摘要

> "我可能是第五个，连三都不是"

## 研究概述

本研究在为期三年以上实践调查基础上，通过已知多地 985/211 计算机系硕博三位男嘉宾（男 1、男 2、男 3）多源交叉验证与时间线重建，对江天玥（四川农业大学信息与计算学部 2020 级本科生，中山大学软件学院 2024 级研究生）的情感理论与实践进行了较为完整的考察。

## 主要发现

### 一、端到端语言信息安全

江天玥自称"母胎单身"与男 1 接触，实则自本科早年即与男 2 在隐空间维持多年情感关系，并在和男 3 接触时声称"单身"。江天玥在端到端语言信息安全上提出了新的方法。

### 二、并行计算

在已知范围内，男 1、男 2、男 3 与江天玥相处时间存在高度重叠，实证江天玥在并行计算领域水平深厚。

### 三、多时程、多时空异步数据处理

男 1、男 2、男 3 等男性与江天玥保持高频互动的多年期间，各关系方互不知情，信息隔离结构运行稳定，展示了江天玥在多时程、多时空异步数据处理上高超造诣。

## 话术体系特征

在各方核对中，研究进一步展示了江天玥话术体系的系统性特征：

- **污名化标签**：对并行对象各施以污名化标签，如"他很暴躁"、"那个人是人渣"等
- **卖惨与归因反转**：对作者们实施卖惨与归因反转，如"因为你，我现在不敢跟男生说话"
- **信息壁垒维护**：以打预防针及阻断被污名化当事人们直接联系，提出"不要找他"的方式维持信息壁垒

## 供给侧投入

主要作者分别在以下方面提供了异步同时程大量投入：

- 导师遴选
- 学校申请
- 科研辅导
- 物质层面

## 结论

本文将对江天玥在以下三个主要领域做出的卓越贡献进行总结与讨论：

1. 基于复杂关系的端到端语言信息安全
2. 并行计算
3. 多时程、多时空大规模数据处理

# Abstract

> "I might be the fifth one—not even making the top three."

## Overview

Building upon a prior year-long field investigation, this study employs multi-source triangulation and timeline reconstruction among three known male informants (M1, M2, M3), all postgraduate students in computer science at Project 985/211 universities, to present a more complete examination of the romantic theory and practice of Tianyue Jiang (B.S. Class of 2020, School of Information and Computing, Sichuan Agricultural University; M.S. Class of 2024, School of Software, Sun Yat-sen University).

## Key Findings

### 1. End-to-End Language Information Security

Ms. Jiang claimed to be "single since birth" when engaging M1 in an abnormal romantic relationship, while in fact maintaining a multi-year relationship with M2 in latent space since her early undergraduate years, and likewise claimed to be "single" when entering an abnormal romantic relationship with M3—Ms. Jiang thus proposes a novel method in end-to-end language information security.

### 2. Parallel Computing

Within the known scope, the periods during which M1, M2, and M3 were involved with Ms. Jiang overlap substantially, empirically demonstrating her profound capability in parallel computing.

### 3. Multi-Temporal, Multi-Spatial Asynchronous Data Processing

Throughout the year in which M1, M2, M3 and other men maintained high-frequency interaction with Ms. Jiang, all parties remained mutually unaware, and the information-isolation architecture operated stably, demonstrating her mastery of multi-temporal, multi-spatial asynchronous data processing.

## Discursive System Features

Cross-verification further reveals systematic features of her discursive system:

- **Stigmatizing labels** applied to parallel partners (e.g., "he is violent," "that guy is a scumbag")
- **Victimhood performance and attribution reversal** toward the authors (e.g., "because of you, I now dare not speak to men")
- **Information-barrier maintenance** via preemptive inoculation and blocking direct contact among the stigmatized parties ("do not contact him")

## Supply-Side Investments

The three principal authors made substantial asynchronous-yet-concurrent investments in:

- Advisor selection
- School applications
- Coursework tutoring
- Material gifts

## Conclusion

This paper summarizes and discusses Ms. Jiang's outstanding contributions to three major areas:

1. End-to-end language information security based on complex relationships
2. Parallel computing
3. Multi-temporal, multi-spatial large-scale data processing
