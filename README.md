# Complex-Relationship-is-All-You-Need-JTY-SYSU-Software
记录了中山大学24级研究生江天玥与横跨华北、华中、华西、华南多地，与多位男嘉宾周旋的技术框架和理论总结

摘要—“我可能是第五个，连三都不是”。本研究在为期三年以上实践调查基础上，通过已知多地 985 211 计算机系硕
博三位男嘉宾男 1, 男 2, 男 3 多源交叉验证与时间线重建，对江天玥（四川农业大学信息与计算学部 2020 级本科生, 中山大学软件学院 24 级研究生）的情感理论与实践进行了为完整的考察。主要发现如下：第一，江天玥自称"母胎
单身"与男 1 接触进入非正常情感关系，实则自本科早年即与男 2 在隐空间维持多年情感关系，并在和男 3 进入非正
常恋爱关系时声称”单身”，江天玥在端到端语言信息安全上提出了新的方法。 第二，在已知范围内，男 1, 男 2, 男 3
与江天玥相处时间存在高度重叠，实证江天玥在并行计算领域水平深厚。第三，男 1, 男 2, 男 3 等男性与江天玥保持
高频互动的多年期间，各关系方互不知情，信息隔离结构运行稳定，展示了江天玥在多时程、多时空异步数据处理
上高超造诣。在各方核对中，研究进一步展示了江天玥话术体系的系统性特征：对并行对象各施以污名化标签如"他
很暴躁", "那个人是人渣"等，对作者们实施卖惨与归因反转，如"因为你，我现在不敢跟男生说话"，并以打预防针及
阻断被污名化当事人们直接联系，提出"不要找他"的方式维持信息壁垒。在供给侧，三名主要作者分别在导师遴
选、学校申请、课程辅导及物质层面提供了异步同时程大量投入。综上，本文将对江天玥在基于复杂关系的端到端
语言信息安全，并行计算，以及多时程、多时空大规模数据处理三个主要领域做出的卓越贡献进行总结与讨论。
关键词：并行计算，信息安全，多时程、多时空大规模数据处理，认知调控，资源调度

Abstract—“I might be the fifth one—not even making the top three.” Building upon a prior year-long field investigation, this
study employs multi-source triangulation and timeline reconstruction among three known male informants (M1, M2, M3), all
postgraduate students in computer science at Project 985/211 universities, to present a more complete examination of the
romantic theory and practice of Tianyue Jiang (B.S. Class of 2020, School of Information and Computing, Sichuan Agricultural University; M.S. Class of 2024, School of Software, Sun Yat-sen University). Key findings are as follows. First, Ms. Jiang claimed to be “single since birth” when engaging M1 in an abnormal romantic relationship, while in fact maintaining a multi-year relationship with M2 in latent space since her early undergraduate years, and likewise claimed to be
“single” when entering an abnormal romantic relationship with M3—Ms. Jiang thus proposes a novel method in end-to-end
language information security. Second, within the known scope, the periods during which M1, M2, and M3 were involved with Ms. Jiang overlap substantially, empirically demonstrating her profound capability in parallel computing. Third, throughout the year in which M1, M2, M3 and other men maintained high-frequency interaction with Ms. Jiang, all parties
remained mutually unaware, and the information-isolation architecture operated stably, demonstrating her mastery of multitemporal, multi-spatial asynchronous data processing. Cross-verification further reveals systematic features of her discursive
system: stigmatizing labels applied to parallel partners (e.g., “he is violent,” “that guy is a scumbag”), victimhood
performance and attribution reversal toward the authors (e.g., “because of you, I now dare not speak to men”), and
information-barrier maintenance via preemptive inoculation and blocking direct contact among the stigmatized parties (“do
not contact him”). On the supply side, the three principal authors made substantial asynchronous-yet-concurrent investments
in advisor selection, school applications, coursework tutoring, and material gifts. In summary, this paper summarizes and
discusses Ms. Jiang’s outstanding contributions to three major areas: end-to-end language information security, parallel
computing, and multi-temporal, multi-spatial large-scale data processing. Keywords: parallel computing, information security, multi-temporal and multi-spatial large-scale data processing, cognitive
regulation，scheduling of resources
